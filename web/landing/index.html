Why Genesis

The portfolio's problem/answer table, in brief.

Facts entered a dozen times

Every portal asks for the same legal name, address and EIN. Genesis captures them once and generates every artifact from the one record.

“Formation bots” that file for you

Scripting the IRS, a bank or Google is barred or illegal. Genesis enforces that line in code and refuses to cross it, whatever calls it.

A domain used as an address

A domain is not a street. The principal place of business is validated, and the filing gate holds every hand-off until it is real.

Launch knowledge in someone's head

The board tracks what is ready, what is blocked and on what, what needs the owner — with an append-only history of both.

What it does

Automated where the stack owns it, prepared where a person must attest.

automated

Number, domain, mail, billing

Zeus orders the business DID · Cerulean registers the zone, publishes the host and issues the wildcard TLS · Oasis creates the branded mailbox · Magnate carries the subscription.

human-attested

Generated PDF packets

Six documents built from the record: the SS-4 packet attaches the official IRS form prefilled from a verified line mapping and lays every value out beside it, plus the bank packet, the verification dossier and one canonical NAP block.

modelled

Tier 1 credit readiness

A published six-factor rubric — identity, file age, banking, tradelines, bureau coverage, utilization. Personal lines are excluded, and every gap names the next honest action.

human-attested

Google, D&B, Experian, Equifax

Each needs a real, verifiable business. Genesis prepares and tracks; the owner claims, submits and attests to their own data.

platform

Authentik SSO, Cerulean Vault

Identity belongs to Cerulean's Authentik and secrets to Cerulean Vault. Genesis stores no password and no long-lived credential.

The boundary

Enforced in code, asserted in tests, checked in CI.

Never automatedBecause
Filing with the IRS The online EIN assistant is restricted to the entity's responsible party. Genesis prefills Form SS-4; the party submits it.
Opening a bank account Account opening requires the signer's identity and beneficial-ownership verification under KYC rules.
A Google Business Profile Google verifies a real, staffed location and suspends listings that have none.
Bureau registrations D&B, Experian and Equifax require the business to attest to its own data.
